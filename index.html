02 · How it works

From link in bio to confirmed slot, in four steps.

01

Drop your link

Drop your link in your Instagram bio. Clients land on your studio page — no app, no signup, no friction.

02

They pick & request

Service, artist, time, deposit — all in one flow. The request hits your dashboard with status "pending."

03

You review & confirm

Approve the deposit receipt with one tap. The booking flips to confirmed and the client gets their code.

04

Show up & work

Both sides know the brief, the time, and the price. No DMs, no missed calls, no awkward back-and-forth.

03 · Features

Every detail, considered.

Not a bloated CRM. Just the things a studio actually needs to run a calm, professional booking flow.

Day view + timeline

Today's bookings as a list, or as a multi-artist vertical timeline. Switch in a tap.

Deposit review queue

Receipts arrive in a single queue. Approve or reject with a note — clients see your reason instantly.

Per-artist availability

Each artist sets their hours, slot length, buffer, and max bookings. The studio composes the calendar.

Brief + reply

Clients send up to 2 references and a note. Artists reply with sketches, message, and a lock-in toggle.

Google Calendar sync

Two-way sync per artist. External events block booking slots; new bookings push to the artist's calendar.

No-account clients

Bookings are saved to the device. A magic email link lets clients pick up where they left off on any phone.

05 · About the maker

Hi, I'm Dabrin. I build the kind of software I'd want to use.

InkSlate started as a side project after watching a friend who runs a tattoo studio drown in DMs every week. The booking apps out there were either built for nail salons in 2014, or generic SaaS that doesn't speak the language of independent studios.

So I'm building something focused: editorial design, a flow that respects everyone's time, and a price that makes sense for a single artist or a small atelier. Built in Costa Rica, from day one.
