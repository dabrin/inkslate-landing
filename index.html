09 — FAQ

What people ask before getting started.

If you still have a question, write us on WhatsApp and we'll answer the same day.

Do my clients have to install an app to book?

+

No. The client opens your studio's link from the browser and books without creating an account or password. If they want, they can also use the iOS or Android app, but it's never required.

Do I have to stop using WhatsApp?

+

No. InkSlate coexists with WhatsApp. Whatever comes in through chat or Instagram, you log manually on the same calendar, so you have one calendar instead of two.

Does it work if I work alone, without a team?

+

Yes. You can have a single artist with your own services, prices and hours. The per-artist calendar works the same when the artist is you.

How do SINPE deposits work?

+

You define which services require a deposit and for how much. The client uploads the receipt from their phone, an automatic assistant pre-reads the amount, date and sender, and you approve or reject with one tap. The final decision is always the studio's.

How much does InkSlate cost in Costa Rica?

+

The first month is free, then it's ₡10,000/month per studio, with no per-booking fee and no contract.

Does it sync with Google Calendar?

+

Yes, both ways: InkSlate bookings show up on your Google Calendar, and whatever you block there is reflected in your availability.

Does it work for barbershops and salons, or only for tattoo studios?

+

It works for any business that runs on appointments. The vocabulary and options change based on business type: the creative brief with reference photos, for example, only turns on for the services that need it. Check out the pages for barbershops, beauty salons and nail artists.

What happens if two people pick the same time slot?

+

It can't happen. As soon as someone picks a slot, it's held for them, with a visible countdown of the time they have to complete the booking.
